## Repos

- Tolar-dio/NBA-Analytics (this one, public): models, site, docs, and the frozen pre-game numbers the site showed. No raw paid data, no raw odds, no wager tracking.
- Tolar-dio/nba-private (private): the BALLDONTLIE pull (`scripts/bdl.py`, `scripts/fetch.py`, workflows `pull.yml` nightly and `snapshot.yml` pre-game), raw data under `bdl/raw/`, table builder and checks (`scripts/tables.py`, `scripts/validate.py`, `bdl/checks/`). This repo's nightly will check out nba-private's raw data with a read-only token and build the tables in the runner, never committing them, the way Ice Sheet reads All Three Zones.

## Data sources (checked Oct 22, 2026)

Primary: BALLDONTLIE, GOAT tier (Tolar subscribed Oct 22, 2026; 600 requests a minute, 5 on the 48-hour trial). Terms allow commercial use, display next to our own projections, storage and archiving, no attribution required; forbidden: a feed that substitutes for their API, sharing the key, implying endorsement. They say they aren't the original producer and third-party rights are the user's responsibility. Their play fields mirror ESPN's feed one for one, so the play-by-play is probably ESPN-derived (unverified). Credit them on About anyway.
- Seasons use BDL's label: 2025 = 2025-26 (their docs say so for advanced stats).
- Play-by-play (`/v1/plays?game_id=`), lineups (starters and bench, once a game begins) and odds exist from 2025-26 on. Plays carry type, text, score, clock, team, `coordinate_x/y` on shots, wallclock and `participants` (player ids); no players-on-court field, so five-man stints get rebuilt from substitutions plus starters.
- `/v1/box_scores?date=` returns every game of a day with player lines (min as a string). Advanced stats v2 (`/nba/v2/stats/advanced`, period 0 = full game) carry possessions, ratings, four factors, usage, and hustle/tracking/matchup numbers (contested shots, defended at the rim, touches, speed, distance, matchup minutes) for full games only.
- Game odds (`/v2/odds`): DraftKings, FanDuel, Caesars, BetMGM, BetRivers, bet365 and more, updated live. Opening game odds and opening player props (`/nba/v2/odds/opening`, `/nba/v2/odds/player_props/opening`) are historical for the last completed season and this one. Current player props (`/v2/odds/player_props?game_id=`) are live only: BDL keeps no history, so the pull's `--snapshot` saves them before tip-off or they're gone. Prop types include points, rebounds, assists, threes, points_rebounds_assists, points_rebounds, points_assists, blocks, steals, double_double, as over/under and milestone (alt line) markets.
- Injuries (`/v1/player_injuries`): current list only (status, return date, description). The pull saves a copy each run; that's our history.

What the first pull showed (Oct 22, 2026: 2025-26 opening week, 53 games, plus 2026-27's first 14):
- The key is on the 48-hour trial's speed: 211 calls took 35 minutes with 35 rate-limit waits (about 6 a minute). A full-season backfill is ~4,400 calls: about 11 minutes at the paid 600 a minute, 15 hours on the trial.
- 2025-26 lists 1,322 games: 1,230 regular (NBA Cup games included), 1 tagged `ist` (the Cup final), 6 play-in, 85 playoffs.
- Game lines: `/v2/odds` for a finished game holds the last in-game number (moneylines like -100000), useless for grading. 2025-26 opening game lines are one book (BetMGM) stamped exactly at tip-off, every game covered: in effect a line at tip, usable as the closing benchmark for the team side. 2026-27 "opening" lines are from August (schedule release), too stale to grade against. From here on, closing lines come from our own snapshots.
- Opening props: 2025-26 has 6 books (BetMGM, Fanatics, BetRivers, DraftKings, FanDuel, Caesars), ~590 lines a game, every one stamped exactly 3 hours before tip: in effect a T-3h snapshot, not true openers. 2026-27 has 9 books, opened 9 to 85 hours before tip, ~3,200 lines a game including first-quarter markets.
- Box minutes are whole minutes in 2025-26 and mm:ss in 2026-27. Our stint minutes match the box within a minute for 100% (2025-26) and 99.4% (2026-27) of player-games.
- Play-by-play is ESPN-shaped: shots carry integer x/y with the hoop at (25, 1); non-shots carry a sentinel (-214748340). Some plays arrive out of order (sort by clock, then feed order); a sub is often logged a beat after a play in the same second by the player coming in; missed end-of-period heaves aren't attempts in the box. Misses don't always say "three point": geometry decides (past 22.5 ft, or 21.5+ ft to the side), fit on 5,552 makes with no exceptions.
- BDL sometimes drops a player's id from a play's `participants` while the text names him (Oct 2026: two Pacers, Monte Morris and Cody Martin, missing from whole games). `tables.py` restores ids from the text using the box score's names: 170 plays in 2025-26's first 497 games.
- Validation on 2025-26 through December (497 games, after that repair): stint minutes within a minute of the box for 99.98% of player-games (worst 2 minutes); play-by-play points match the final in all but 3 games (feed gaps); player FGA mismatches 59 of 10,858, 3PA 95, points 16, FTA 9.
- Validation (`scripts/validate.py` in nba-private), first week: every box total equals the final; play-by-play team points equal the final in all but 2 fresh 2026-27 games (LAL-GSW 130 vs 127, PHX at POR 82 vs 83; days refetch until 30 hours after tip, so watch whether they correct); player FGA mismatches 9 of 1,197 and 1 of 322, 3PA 7 and 4, points 4 and 0 (a free throw or basket credited to a teammate).

Ruled out by their own terms (read Oct 22, 2026), whether or not they answer:
- NBA.com (cdn.nba.com, stats.nba.com). Terms of use section 9 "NBA Statistics" (updated July 13, 2026): news reporting or private non-commercial use only; not in connection with any gambling activity, including legal gambling; not in a product presenting a live or archived play-by-play account; not in a site featuring a database of full, regularly updated NBA stats without the league's consent. Also cdn.nba.com returned Akamai 403 to the sandbox for every User-Agent, and stats.nba.com hung with an honest one (it wants browser headers; sending them would be spoofing).
- ESPN APIs (site.api.espn.com, sports.core.api.espn.com). Not used here. (The same clause covers Ice Sheet's ESPN layer; that's Tolar's call, flagged to him Oct 22, 2026.)

Later, if needed: The Odds API (display on a site allowed, including commercial; storage and derived values allowed; no redistributing raw files, so raw odds stay private). $30/month for 20k credits; historical event odds cost 10 x markets x regions; props history from May 2023. ## Analysis (public code, private data)

- `analysis/common.py`: tables are read from `private/bdl/tables/` (locally a symlink to a clone of nba-private, in the nightly a read-only checkout; `NBA_TABLES` overrides). Outputs go to `analysis/output/<season>/` (gitignored). Seasons: `PREV, CUR = 2025, 2026`.
- `analysis/shot_quality.py <seasons>`: expected points per shot. Gradient-boosted classifier (sklearn HistGradientBoosting) on where (distance, distance to the side, y, 2/3, corner three), what (shot-description flags: dunk, layup, tip, putback, alley-oop, hook, floater, bank, fadeaway, turnaround, step-back, pull-up, running, driving, cutting, finger roll, reverse) and context (seconds since the possession started and how: defensive rebound, steal, other turnover, opponent make or free throw, period start; putback within 3 s of his team's offensive rebound; any second chance; score margin; period; last 4 seconds). Out of fold, grouped by game. Never assisted/blocked (known only after). Heaves valued at the league heave rate.
- Shot model on the full 2025-26 season (Oct 23, 2026; 234,883 shots, playoffs included, out of fold): log loss boosted 0.6362, logistic 0.6438, zone x shot description 0.6414, zone only 0.6552 (AUC 0.664 / 0.652 / 0.656 / 0.640). Calibrated within about a point in every zone. Steals start the best shots (57% makes, 1.25 expected points), opponent free throws the worst (44%). The site trains on 2025-26 and 2026-27 together (0.6358).
- Leak found and fixed on the way: rebounds logged before their miss made "followed an offensive rebound" predict misses (the next shot went in 3%). With the leak the boosted model scored 0.6323 on the first 497 games; the honest number was 0.6374. Check any new context feature for the same kind of ordering leak.
- Expected points per trip (`xpts_trip`, `trips()` in shot_quality.py). Summing every shot's expected points credits a team twice for one possession when it misses and rebounds: Portland's 107-shot, 26-offensive-rebound loss to Chicago (Nov 19, 2025) came out 149 expected points to 121 actual. Each possession now counts its first shot at its own quality plus, if it misses, the team's rate of getting a second-chance shot (season rate, shrunk toward the league with 50 misses; league 23% of first-shot misses) times the league's second-chance points (1.30). Season totals match what went in (255,915 vs 255,602 field-goal points). Tested on full 2025-26 split halves (30 teams): first-half actual net predicts second-half net at r 0.771; from shots, raw sum 0.699, sequential (later shots discounted by earlier misses, which runs 6% low in total) 0.688, per trip 0.705; 70/30 blends of actual and any of them 0.78-0.785. Within noise, so per trip was chosen for coherence: on game pages it picks the winner 66.7% of the time, expected and actual margins correlate 0.50, and totals are unbiased. Per-shot `xpts` stays for shot-making and expected points per shot.
- `analysis/teams.py <season>`: regular season only (the NBA Cup final doesn't count, as in the standings). Pace, ratings per 100 possessions, four factors for and against, opponent 3P% and FT% against league, shot quality for and against, shot-making above expectation, expected net rating from `xpts_trip`.
- `analysis/players.py <season>`: per team stint (five-man stretch) its and its opponent's FGA, FTA, offensive rebounds (players only; team rebounds after a missed first free throw left out), turnovers (any play type containing "Turnover" except "No Turnover", plus "Traveling": matches the box for 99.8% of player-games; "Offensive Charge" is the foul, its turnover is logged separately), points and expected points per trip. Possessions per stint average both sides; stint totals run 0.7 a game above the box estimate because team turnovers count. Outputs `players.csv` (box rates, usage, TS%, shot mix by zone, expected points per shot, shot-making, assisted share, on/off actual and from shots, BDL tracking: rim shots defended and FG% allowed, contests, deflections, touches), `player_games.csv.gz` (usage per game), `lineups.csv` (units with 40+ possessions, names by last name with first names for teammates who share one, ordered by minutes), `stint_stats.csv.gz`. Our usage correlates 0.86 with BDL's `usage_percentage` (10+ games).
- Zones used on the site: rim (under 4 ft), short (4-14 ft), midrange twos (14 ft+), corner threes (21.5+ ft to the side, y <= 9), above-the-break threes.

## The site (site/)

- v0 shipped Oct 23, 2026: League, Teams, Players, Games, plus About from the footer (`site/about.js`: about, methods, glossary, data, privacy, gambling). News and the live layer followed the same day (sections below); Tonight, Track record and Pulse come with phases 3-5.
- `site/build.py` writes `site/data/` (gitignored) for each season with outputs: `meta.json`, `league.<s>.json`, `players.<s>.json`, `player/<s>/<id>.json` (bio, lines, game log, shots as [x, y, made, three, p_make x1000, game index], zones vs league), `team/<s>/<abbr>.json` (players, lineups, results with expected points and win chance, zone profile for and against), `games.<s>.json`, `game/<s>/<id>.json` (quarters, box, shots with per-trip expected points, free throws, scoring flow, win chance). "Shots said" (win chance) is an exact convolution of per-trip point distributions: first shot at its value and odds, else a second chance at the team's rate scoring 2 or 3 at league rates; free throws as they happened.
- Front end: plain JS, `site/app.js` (router, pages), `site/court.js` (half court in feet, hoop at the top, O for makes, X for misses, red X for misses on 60%+ looks, nearest-shot picking), `site/style.css`. Design: a coach's clipboard. Light theme is a whiteboard (#f3f5f5 board, white sheets, aluminum frame #b3bbc2 under the masthead), dark theme a chalkboard (#18201d). Two markers: blue for makes, good numbers and links; red for bad numbers and warnings. Archivo (variable width; condensed 68-72% for headings) for everything, Kalam only for hand-written notes on charts. Season switch in the masthead (`board-season`), your team (`board-team`), follows (`board-follow`), theme (`board-theme`), all localStorage. Early-season warning under 300 games.
- `.github/workflows/site.yml` (public repo, free minutes): checks out nba-private's `bdl/raw` and `scripts` into `private/` with the `PRIVATE_DATA_TOKEN` secret (read-only), builds the tables in the runner (never committed), runs shot_quality (both seasons), teams, players, build, and deploys to GitHub Pages. Logs are public, so every analysis step's output goes to /dev/null. Started by nba-private's nightly through its `DISPATCH_TOKEN` secret (Actions write on NBA-Analytics); fallback schedule 13:53 UTC. A full run takes about 3 minutes of compute locally.
- Local preview: `ln -s <nba-private clone> private`, run the pipeline (see site.yml), then `cd site && python -m http.server`. Screenshots with Playwright (Chromium is preinstalled in Claude's sandbox).

## Live (Oct 23, 2026)

- `worker/live.js`: a Cloudflare Worker (free plan: 100,000 requests a day, 10 ms CPU; parsing a full game's plays takes about 2 ms) holding `BDL_API_KEY` as a Worker secret. `/scores` (yesterday's and today's games, Eastern dates), `/game?id=` (header, both box scores from `/v1/box_scores/live` matched by home team and date, every play trimmed to [order, period, clock, side, kind, made, value, x, y, player, home score, away score, shot type, heave]), `/injuries`. Answers are kept 15-20 s in isolate memory and Cloudflare's cache (the cache may be a no-op on workers.dev), CORS limited to tolar-dio.github.io and localhost:8765 (`ALLOWED_ORIGINS`), errors never echo upstream details. Offline test: `node worker/test_live.mjs <fixtures>` (fixtures are BDL-shaped files built from nba-private raw; not committed).
- The site gets the relay's URL from the `LIVE_URL` repository variable through site.yml into `meta.live_url`; blank means no live layer. `site/live.js`: score strip under the masthead (polls /scores every 60 s while games are on or about to start, 5 minutes otherwise, pauses in hidden tabs), Today on the Games tab, `#live/<id>` (score, quarters, what the shots say so far with the margin chart stopped at the last play, both shot charts, box scores; refreshes every 30 s). Live shots are valued with `data/shotvalue.json` (make rate by zone x shot description, shrunk to the zone with 30 shots; the same table scored 0.641 log loss against the model's 0.636) and counted per trip with each team's second-chance rate, as on game pages.
- Budget: a visitor watching a game page all evening makes about 500 relay requests, so the free tier covers roughly 200 such visitors a day. If it ever runs out the strip just disappears; Workers Paid ($5/month) lifts it to 10 million.

## Rules carried over from Ice Sheet

- Every model change is tested walk-forward (each night predicted only from earlier games) on last season, then checked on this season, and adopted only if it holds in both. Record what failed here as well as what worked. Check calibration by role (starters vs bench, minutes bands).
- Freeze every published number at tip-off, grade it the next morning, show it on the Track record page, including against sportsbook lines. Note: BDL's history is opening lines, which are softer than closing lines; beating the open is a lower bar than beating the close, so the live record (our own pre-tip snapshots) is the real test.
- Product: for every NBA fan, no default team, visitor picks a team and follows players (localStorage), times in the visitor's zone, About page (methods, glossary, data credits, AI, responsible gambling, privacy). Voice: "we" to "you", plain, explains terms, no build internals.
- Sleepers chosen on role, process and matchup, never "he's due", each with reasons and our probability, graded publicly.
- AI writing (recaps, previews, news takes) with the Claude API, model claude-sonnet-5-5, facts from our data only, no invented quotes or feelings, every AI piece labelled. No em dashes in house style.
- No scraping sites whose terms forbid it, no spoofed headers, no rehosted video (link official clips; embed only the NBA's own YouTube uploads), headlines and teasers only from news sources, credit every data source, betting copy informational with the 1-800-GAMBLER line.
